ABOUT THE RESEARCH

A focused industry research project.

Equipment Dealer Research conducts focused research into the operational realities of equipment dealerships.

Our current study is based on conversations with industry professionals and is intended to identify recurring problems, useful patterns and areas where dealerships may be leaving time or money on the table.

Participation is voluntary. We do not require confidential customer information or proprietary dealership data to take part.
